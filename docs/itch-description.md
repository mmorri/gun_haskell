## Title

Pew-Pew Defense Deluxe

## Short tagline (itch uses ~one line under the title)

A tiny tower defense game about gremlins, questionable turrets, and one very anxious crystal.

## Description

**The gremlins are coming. They are extremely rude.**

Pew-Pew Defense Deluxe is a compact, hand-drawn tower defense game. Gremlins pour out of a
mysterious hole in space, follow a suspiciously convenient road, and try to snack on your
crystal. You have six kinds of turret, a limited budget, and a growing sense of dread.

Build. Upgrade. Sell. Regret. Repeat.

### What's in it

- **6 turrets, each with a personality problem**
  - **Pew-Pew** — reliable, like socks
  - **BRRRT** — caffeine incarnate, spins its barrels for morale
  - **Nanny** — 360 no-scope granny, high range, slow
  - **YEET** — a diplomatic solution with splash damage
  - **Frosty** — emotional cold shoulder, slows everything down
  - **Tesla** — shocking, honestly; chains between nearby gremlins
- **Two superpowers, for when the road is too full**
  - **A-10 Warthog (Q)** — draw a start and end point; the plane strafes the whole line
  - **Marine Drop (E)** — five marines, five seconds of completely unreasonable fire rate
- **Gremlins with feelings** — zoomies, espresso goblins, armoured chunguses, and the
  occasional MEGA CHUNGUS boss
- **Meta-progression** — earn teeth, spend them in the Gremlin Lab on permanent upgrades
- **11 achievements** for people who like numbers going up
- **Procedural audio** — every pew, brrrt, and explosion is generated in the browser
- **Keyboard + mouse, touch-friendly**, with 2× speed for the impatient
- **No installs, no accounts, no ads.** Runs in the browser.

### Controls

- **Click** a turret card, then click the map to build
- **1–6** pick a turret · **right-click** cancels placement
- **Space** starts the wave · **F** toggles 2× speed · **Esc** pauses
- **Q** calls an A-10 strafing run (click start, then end) · **E** drops 5 marines
- Click a placed turret to upgrade, sell, or change targeting
- Everything also works with touch

### How long is it?

A run takes about 10–20 minutes. A good run goes a lot longer. The game saves your
progress locally in your browser, so upgrades and achievements stick around.
